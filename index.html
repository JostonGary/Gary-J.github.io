About

I am a computational social scientist at GOVCOPP – Research Unit on Governance, Competitiveness and Public Policies, where I build machine learning and statistical models for large-scale behavioral and social data. I am completing my Ph.D. at Linköping University, Sweden and University of Aveiro, University of Minho, Portugal.

My work is method-led: I develop measurement and modeling strategies — structural equation modeling, machine learning hybrids, meta-analysis, and text and multimodal analysis — and apply them across substantive domains, from digital platforms and tourism to environmental and public health behavior.

I welcome collaboration on computational approaches to social and consumer behavior. Feel free to email me.
