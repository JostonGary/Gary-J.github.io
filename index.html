About

Joston Gary is a Visiting Ph.D. Student and Affiliated Researcher at Linköping University, supervised by Prof. Lars Witell.

His primary research area is computational social science. His research combines machine learning, statistical modeling, and computational methods to study human behavior and decision-making using large-scale and digitally generated data. His work spans several application domains, including consumer behavior, digital technology, tourism, health sciences, sustainability, and environmental research, with a common focus on understanding social and behavioral processes through computational methods.
